01 / HOW TO USE

Three steps to a clean cutout.

  1. 01Upload an image with a solid color screen.
  2. 02Choose a color, or sample it from the image.
  3. 03Fine-tune the edge, then download your PNG.

02 / FORMATS & LIMITS

Made for still images.

Input: PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, and BMP. Output: transparent PNG. Large images may take longer or exceed your browser's available memory. Results depend on how evenly the screen is lit.

VIDEO Video files can be selected, but transparent video export is not available in this version.

03 / QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

What does “green screen background remover” mean?

It is another name for a chroma key tool that makes a selected background color transparent.

Can I remove a color other than green?

Yes. Pick blue, choose any custom color, or sample a pixel directly from your image.

Does this upload my image?

No. The image is processed in this page using your browser's Canvas API.
